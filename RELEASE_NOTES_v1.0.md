# AHIP Public-Benefit Toolkit - Release Notes

## Version 1.0 - July 2026

Initial reviewed release containing:

- A concise AHIP public overview and national replication framework.
- A Miami-Dade County permitting proof of concept with documented interpretation controls.
- A housing analytics reference guide covering official sources, KPIs, data quality, and local adaptation.
- A responsible-use and public-access guide covering human review, limitations, versioning, and information boundaries.
- PDF and editable DOCX versions of all three documents.
- A selected AHIP dashboard image for public explanation.

### Claims and boundaries

- No government adoption, endorsement, partnership, or national deployment is claimed.
- Miami-Dade is a demonstration geography, not the geographic limit of AHIP.
- Application-to-issuance time is total elapsed calendar time and must not automatically be described as agency review time.
- The toolkit supports professional review and does not replace legal, policy, engineering, underwriting, appraisal, funding, permitting, or community decision authority.
